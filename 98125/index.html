05 · What It Means

Three things to take away.

01

Light rail rewrote the map.

The new 130th/Pinehurst and 145th stations are visible in pricing power. Pinehurst, Victory Heights, and Olympic Hills are quietly converging with neighborhoods that historically traded above them.

02

Buyers reward finished work.

"Remodeled" or "updated" appears in 45% of listing remarks, and homes flagged "Very Good" or "Remodeled" condition cleared at the highest sale-to-list ratios. Pre-listing prep keeps paying.

03

Outdoor + flex space still wins.

Fenced yards, patios, decks, and a usable office/bonus room remain the most-cited features. Two-story-with-basement and 1-story-with-basement homes commanded the highest medians.
